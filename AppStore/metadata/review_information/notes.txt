LaWallet is a non-custodial Lightning Network (bitcoin) wallet for communities. Funds live in Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) wallets; the app signs requests with a key that stays on the device. It sells no digital goods or features and contains no in-app purchases.

HOW TO TEST
1. Launch → "Create account" → "Create a random key" → confirm you backed up the secret key → Continue → Enter.
2. Claim a Lightning address (any free username). The community server assigns a wallet automatically.
3. Home shows the balance, Receive shows the address QR and amount invoices, Send pays an address or invoice (swipe to pay).

PERMISSIONS
• Camera: scanning Lightning invoices, addresses and card activation QR codes (Scan button in the tab bar).
• NFC: reading Bolt Cards (NTAG 424) to claim sats or pay; requires a physical card.
• Face ID: passkey sign-in, handled by iOS.
• Notifications: local notifications when a payment is received. No push server.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings → Security → Delete account.
