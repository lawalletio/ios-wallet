LaWallet gives you a Lightning address, a Nostr identity and a bitcoin wallet in one app, run by your community.

Get paid with an address as easy as an email — you@yourcommunity — and pay anyone on the Lightning Network in seconds.

SEND AND RECEIVE
• Pay Lightning addresses, LNURL and invoices with a swipe
• Receive to your address or with an amount-specific invoice
• Scan any Lightning QR code, including withdraw links to claim sats
• Saved contacts and recipient suggestions
• Live balance and activity, with a notification when a payment lands

YOUR KEYS, YOUR IDENTITY
• Create an account in seconds with a passkey or a new Nostr key
• Log in with your nsec, a passkey or a remote signer (NIP-46 bunker)
• Back up your secret key at any time; it never leaves your device

BOLT CARDS
• Activate a community card by scanning its QR code
• Tap a card on your iPhone to claim or pay
• Manage your cards and choose which wallet they spend from

BRING YOUR OWN WALLET
• Connect any Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) wallet
• Forward incoming payments and send webhook or Nostr notifications

MADE FOR COMMUNITIES
• Works with any LaWallet community — switch instance in Settings
• Shows amounts in sats, BTC or your local currency
• English and Spanish

LaWallet is open source (github.com/lawalletio). It does not show ads or track you.
